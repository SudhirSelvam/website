🔊 The Taos Hum

A Low-Frequency Mystery

The Taos Hum is a mysterious, low-frequency sound that has been heard by residents in the small town of Taos, New Mexico, for decades. Described as a constant hum or buzz, it is often likened to the sound of an idling diesel engine or a distant motor running.

Despite numerous investigations by researchers and scientists, the source of the hum remains elusive. While some believe that the sound may be caused by industrial machinery or even underground geological activity, others propose that it could be the result of electromagnetic waves or even some form of government experimentation.

Only a small percentage of people in Taos report hearing the hum, leading some to speculate that it may be a psychosomatic phenomenon, caused by environmental factors like low-frequency sound waves. However, the mystery remains unsolved, with no definitive explanation for the strange and persistent noise.
